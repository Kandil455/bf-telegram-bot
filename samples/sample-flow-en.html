Price elasticity of demand

Intro microeconomics · Lecture 7

8 Oct 2026

Definition

Price elasticity of demand
Percentage change in quantity demanded divided by the percentage change in price.Example: Price rises 10%, quantity falls 20%: elasticity = −2.
Midpoint formulaE = [(Q2 − Q1) / ((Q1+Q2)/2)] ÷ [(P2 − P1) / ((P1+P2)/2)]Use when prices change a lot.

Classes of elasticity

  • Necessities tend to be inelastic.
  • Luxuries and close substitutes tend to be elastic.
  • Longer time horizons make demand more elastic.
⚠ Exam trap Elasticity is not the slope of the demand curve. Slope depends on units; elasticity does not.

Steps to compute

  1. Write down the two prices and the two quantities.
  2. Compute the percentage change using the midpoint method.
  3. Divide the quantity change by the price change.
  4. Read the sign and the absolute value.
Why use the midpoint formula?
It gives the same answer whether you move from A to B or from B to A.
Is a negative sign a problem?
No. The law of demand makes it negative; compare absolute values.
